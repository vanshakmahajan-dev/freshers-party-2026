About the Party

A welcome event organised by the Students' Committee for every incoming student of SPMR College of Commerce.

Who it is for

All first-year students and their families. Students bring their college ID; guests are welcome with an entry pass.

What to expect

An evening of music, dance and cultural acts from across the state, plus food stalls open throughout the night.

Dress code

Smart casual is encouraged. Traditional and ethnic outfits are welcome — many students perform in them.

Event Schedule

A rough running order for the day.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Arrival & Entry

    Guest check-in and collection of entry passes.

  2. 9:30 AM

    Welcome Address

    Principal's remarks, an alumni address and the lamp-lighting ceremony.

  3. 10:00 AM

    Fresher's Performance

    Opening act by the first-year batch, including the flash mob.

  4. 1:00 PM

    Lunch

    Food stalls open, with folk music on the College Ground.

  5. 2:00 PM

    Live Music & Cultural Performances

    Classical dance, the drama sketch and the headline band.

  6. 3:00 PM

    Photo Session

    Group photographs at the College Ground.

  7. 4:00 PM

    Closing

    Thank-you address and the closing note.
